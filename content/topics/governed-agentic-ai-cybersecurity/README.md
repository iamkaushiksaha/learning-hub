# Governed Agentic AI for Cybersecurity

## Audience

Cybersecurity practitioners and leaders who understand security delivery but
may be new to agentic AI engineering.

## Learning promise

Move from familiar chat usage to reusable skills, bounded agents, security
controls, governed orchestration, and measurable outcomes without hiding human
accountability behind AI terminology.

## Published experience

- Four one-hour visual sessions.
- Progressive reveals, decision prompts, flow diagrams, and speaker notes.
- A cybersecurity orchestrator case study and a build-and-secure workshop.
- XAI is reserved for a later clinic so the foundation series remains focused.

## Canonical artifact

The interactive presentation is consolidated under
`public/presentations/governed-agentic-ai/` in this repository. The hub topic is
its discovery and context page; `content/topic-map.md` connects every teaching
segment to deeper technical-learning pages.

The recommended one-hour route is the 14-page simplified session under
`public/presentations/governed-agentic-ai/simplified/`. Its format-neutral
content, diagram, animation, speaker-note, reference and PowerPoint production
specification is maintained in
`content/presentations/governed-agentic-ai-simplified-spec.md`. The original
28-page summary remains available as the detailed technical route.
