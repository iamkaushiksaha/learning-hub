# Governed Agentic AI for Cybersecurity

## Audience

Cybersecurity practitioners and leaders who understand security delivery but
may be new to agentic AI engineering.

## Learning promise

Move from familiar chat usage to reusable skills, bounded agents, security
controls, governed orchestration, and measurable outcomes without hiding human
accountability behind AI terminology.

## Published experience

- Four one-hour visual sessions.
- Progressive reveals, decision prompts, flow diagrams, and speaker notes.
- A cybersecurity orchestrator case study and a build-and-secure workshop.
- XAI is reserved for a later clinic so the foundation series remains focused.
